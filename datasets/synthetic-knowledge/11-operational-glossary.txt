ENGLISH GLOSSARY OF OPERATIONAL EVIDENCE - GLO-011

Aurora Nebula is a fictional organization. All people, systems, figures and incidents in this corpus are invented for software testing. This glossary supports Portuguese and English semantic retrieval without relying on confidential enterprise documents.

Evidence is an observed document excerpt with a verifiable reference. A reference should identify an existing document, an existing chunk and its source. A similarity score expresses vector proximity; it does not prove that a claim is true. A target is not a measured outcome. The fictional search availability target in KPI-003 is 99.5 percent and the p95 search latency objective is below 800 milliseconds.

Idempotent replay preserves the original document identifier when normalized content and indexing configuration are unchanged. The same operation can be retried without creating another copy. Incident JASMIM-17 in INC-007 lasted 17 minutes and required replay of 240 documents. These are declared scenario facts, not live measurements collected from the application.

A recovery time objective describes the maximum desired restoration delay. A recovery point objective describes the maximum desired data loss window. BCP-009 sets them to 30 minutes and 5 minutes respectively. Insufficient evidence is a valid retrieval status and should never be converted into a fabricated source. A deterministic report is rule-based output, and must not be described as autonomous language-model inference.
